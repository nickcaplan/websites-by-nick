Hire the barn for your event

All our spaces and facilities are available for hire, with audio visual facilities and on-site catering.

  • Weddings and celebrations, with your own caterers or the Riverhouse Café
  • A fully-flexible performance space with stage lighting and surround sound
  • Seminars, meetings and education, with discounted rates for educational and non-profit projects
  • The Robert Phillips Gallery for artists and community shows

The Community Café

A not-for-profit café powered by volunteers, cooking with surplus food that would otherwise go to landfill. Open daily 10am-4pm for breakfast, lunch, light bites, coffees and cakes, with a licensed bar opening about 45 minutes before evening events.

Getting here and access

Plenty of on-site parking and bike racks. Walton station is a 20-minute walk, with buses 555 and 458 to the town centre, and local taxis available.

Wheelchair access covers the first-floor Gallery, the Barn, the Café, the Learning Studio and toilets, and a hearing loop is fitted. Tell us any special requirements when booking and we will do our best to assist.
