This notice explains how this website uses information and optional analytics cookies.

Website enquiries

Nick Caplan uses the information you send through the contact form to read your enquiry and respond. The form sends your name, email address and message to Formspree. Please do not include unnecessary sensitive information.

Read Formspree's privacy policy for information about its service.

Cookie settings

Google Analytics is optional. It is not loaded and no analytics requests are made unless you choose Allow analytics. You can reject cookies and still use the whole site.

Analytics cookies

With your permission, Google Analytics measures visits and page use to help improve this website. It uses cookies such as _ga to recognise returning browsers. Advertising features are disabled. This site does not deliberately send contact form fields to Google Analytics.

Read Google's privacy policy for information about how Google handles data.

Your choice is remembered in this browser's local storage. To change it or withdraw consent, use Cookie preferences above, available through Privacy in every page's footer. Withdrawing consent stops further analytics and removes this site's analytics cookies. When your choice can be saved, the page reloads to unload the analytics script. If your browser blocks saving a rejection or withdrawal, analytics stays disabled on the current page. If you allow analytics but that choice cannot be saved, analytics is allowed on the current page only; you will need to choose again when you return. A rejection is also kept in a first-party fallback cookie and session storage where available, and sent to other open tabs. A visible warning explains a failed save. If all browser storage and tab messaging are blocked, close other tabs and check your choice when you return. It does not delete information already sent to Google.

Display preferences

Your light or dark theme choice is stored locally in your browser. It is not used for analytics. Local and development previews can also remember a display-size choice. Clearing this site's browser data removes these preferences and your cookie choice.
