About TALK

Established in 1997, TALK provides long-term support and communication opportunities for people with aphasia. We run groups in four locations across Surrey, serving approximately 80 people with aphasia and their families.

Our mission is to support and empower people to live positively with aphasia, through engagement with their local peer network and the wider community.

Friendly trained volunteers are always present, and the relaxed atmosphere promotes lots of laughter and fun.

What is aphasia?

Aphasia is a language and communication disorder. It is usually caused by damage to the left side of the brain, and the most common cause is stroke.

People with aphasia often have trouble with:

  • speaking
  • understanding speech
  • reading and writing

Aphasia affects each person differently.
