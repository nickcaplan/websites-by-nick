Navigation options

Design review only, not linked from the site. Each option shows the header on a phone with four pages (today) and with six (a sample: Services and Journal are placeholders, not planned pages), and on desktop. It follows the saved light or dark theme.

Current (for reference)

Boxed chips on phones. Shown with four pages, then with six: the header grows with every page.

A - Underlined tabs

One line of plain text tabs under the name, active page underlined. Swipes sideways when there are more pages than fit, so the header never gets taller. Same style as the screenshot tabs on the Work page.

B - Menu button

A single Menu button beside the controls opens a list of every page. The header stays one row at any number of pages, at the cost of one extra tap. Shown closed, then open with six pages (the buttons really open and close).

C - Text links that wrap

Plain small-caps links in a wrapped row under the name, with a thin line under the active page and no boxes. Costs one or two lines and grows slowly with more pages.

D - Bottom bar

Phone only: the pages sit in a bar fixed to the bottom of the screen, within thumb reach, and the top stays just the name and controls. Past four pages the extras go under More (it opens). Desktop keeps the top links.
