About Ben

Ben Ash is a professional guitarist and teacher based in Walton-on-Thames. He holds an MA in music, is a Dean Guitars artist and mentors the next generation of musicians at WaterBear college.

As a performer he plays with Phobetor and Strigoi, and brings that real-world band experience into every lesson.
