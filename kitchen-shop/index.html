About us

A Weybridge institution for over 30 years

Walk through the door and the first impression is of an Aladdin's Cave: an overwhelming selection of cookware, kitchen equipment, utensils, gadgets and gifts.

From everyday kitchen necessities to dinner-party tableware and celebrity-chef-endorsed products, our extensive range satisfies most requirements - and we are always happy to source more unusual items.

Our staff are experienced and friendly: happy to give help and advice, or to simply let you browse.

Nick, Gemma and the team look forward to welcoming you.
