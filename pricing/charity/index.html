Charity

Free websites for small local charities.

If you run a small registered charity and your website is holding you back, or you don’t have one, I take on a limited number of charity projects free of charge each year, whether that’s a new website or a repair.

What the offer covers

  • A simple, fast one-page website, designed and built with you, using your words and pictures
  • Repairs to your existing website when a rebuild is more than you need
  • Your charity keeps ownership of its domain and hosting; any ongoing running costs stay with you and are agreed in writing before anything starts
  • Places are limited, and paid client work comes first

To apply, get in touch with your charity’s name, registration number and what’s not working today.
