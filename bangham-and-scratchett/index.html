Key cutting

Our story

From Ashford to Sunbury

Quality Cobbler was first set up in Ashford, Middlesex in 1998. In 2024 we moved with Shaw Trophies to our new premises at 191 Vicarage Road in Sunbury on Thames, to form Bangham and Scratchett of London Limited. Our owner and director has been in the shoe repair trade for some 40 years and more - and as you can imagine, has pretty much seen it all.
