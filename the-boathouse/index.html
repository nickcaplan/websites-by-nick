Our coffee

From the day we first opened our doors, we have been working with and serving the finest speciality coffee beans to our customers.

Every cup is made with the same care, whether it is your first flat white of the morning or an afternoon pick-me-up by the river.

Our food

We offer a range of tasty hot and cold food, all handmade and absolutely delicious. Indulge yourself in a crusty bacon roll, a toastie, a fresh sandwich or a panini for breakfast, brunch or lunch.

Or treat yourself to a slice of one of our fabulous handmade cakes. Only if you deserve it though!

The little details
