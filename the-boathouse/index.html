Coffee and food

What we serve

From the day we first opened our doors, we have been working with and serving the finest speciality coffee beans - alongside food made by hand, here.

Our coffee

Speciality coffee beans, sourced and served with care. Whether it is your morning flat white on the way past the marina or a slow afternoon pot with a view of the river, the coffee is the heart of what we do.

Breakfast, brunch and lunch

Crusty bacon rolls, toasties, fresh sandwiches and paninis - tasty hot and cold food, all handmade and absolutely delicious.

Handmade cakes and treats

A slice of one of our fabulous handmade cakes - only if you deserve it though.

Sweets and treats

Our mission is to source and serve the finest quality produce - speciality coffee, sandwiches, sweets and treats in the heart of Walton on the River Thames.

About The Boathouse

Modern-rustic, riverside, and dog friendly

The Boathouse is a modern-rustic coffee shop with lovely views of the River Thames, serving specialist coffee, hot and cold food as well as fabulous handmade cakes and treats. As our name suggests, this is a place shaped by the river - coffee and boat hire at Walton Marina, est 2019.
