Company history

Building Molesey since the railway age

Jesse Wheatley started in business as a builder and decorator in 1850 in neighbouring Walton-on-Thames, and came to Molesey in 1856. As the railway brought demand for housing, Wheatleys built much of East Molesey - including Beauchamp Road, Palace Road, Wolsey Road, Matham Road and Arnison Road - joined by Jesse's four sons, with a second office in Esher High Street. The company has long had a close association with the Church, including involvement in building the St Paul's Church spire in the late nineteenth century.
